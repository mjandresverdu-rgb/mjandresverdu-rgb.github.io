Kbolo IPTV privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Versión en español

Kbolo IPTV is a video player. It includes no channels, movies or any other content: it only plays the playlists (M3U or Xtream Codes) that the user adds.

Data the app stores

Playlist addresses, Xtream Codes usernames and passwords, favorites, folder order and playback position are stored only on the device, in the app's private storage. Android backup is disabled for the app, so this data is not uploaded to the cloud.

Data that is sent

The app only connects to the servers the user enters: it downloads the playlist, the program guide (EPG), the logos and the video from them. Those servers belong to third parties and have their own policies.

We do not collect or receive any data: the app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it shares no information with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete playlists from inside the app, or everything at once by uninstalling it or from Settings → Apps → Kbolo IPTV → Storage → Clear data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
