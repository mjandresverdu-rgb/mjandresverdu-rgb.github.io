Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

In one line

Golmetria has no accounts and asks you for no personal data. The only things that leave your phone are the requests that download the predictions, and the data Google needs to show the advertising that pays for the app.

This policy explains what data the Golmetria app for Android handles, why, and for how long. The data controller is Francisco Filiu, contactable at ffiliugomez@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

The app has no sign-up and no login. We never ask for, or receive, any of the following:

  • Name, email address or phone number.
  • Precise device location.
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar or microphone.
  • Financial or payment data. The app charges nothing and has no in-app purchases.

What is handled

1. Requests to the Golmetria server

To show you predictions, statistics and league tables, the app queries our server. Like any internet server, its technical logs record the IP address the request came from, the path requested, and the date and time.

Those logs exist so the service runs and so faults can be diagnosed. Because the app has no accounts, they are not linked to any identified person — there is nothing to cross-reference them against. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and maintaining the service, and they are kept only briefly, for as long as that diagnosis needs.

The content the server returns is public and identical for everyone. There is no personalised content and therefore no profiling on our side.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app is free and is funded by advertising served through Google AdMob. To display and measure it, Google may process:

  • Your Android device's advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, and the approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device information: model, operating system, language.
  • Your interactions with the ads.

That processing is carried out by Google under its own terms. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device.

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows you a message before displaying any advertising so that you can decide how your data is used for advertising purposes. That message is handled by Google's consent management platform.

You can change your decision at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app. If you withdraw consent the app keeps working exactly the same; what changes is that the advertising is no longer personalised.

4. Data that never leaves your device

The app stores a few things in local storage on your phone, and none of them is sent anywhere:

  • Which predictions you have already been notified about, so the alert is not repeated.
  • Ad frequency counters, so you are not shown more than you should be.
  • Your advertising consent decision.

All of it is erased when you uninstall the app.

5. Notifications

New-prediction alerts are generated on your own device: the app periodically queries the server and, if something is new, creates the notification locally. We use no push notifications, no device identifier is registered in our systems, and we do not use Firebase Cloud Messaging.

You can turn notifications off in Android settings without affecting anything else in the app.

No analytics tooling

The app includes no analytics or behavioural tracking SDK — not Google Analytics, not Firebase Analytics, none from third parties. Beyond the advertising described above, nobody measures what you do inside the app.

Children

Golmetria deals with sports prediction content and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

If the General Data Protection Regulation or the UK GDPR applies to you, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

With one caveat worth stating plainly: because there are no accounts and no identifiers tied to people, we cannot in practice locate "your" data among our technical logs, since it is not linked to you. What is entirely in your hands is managing advertising consent from within the app, and resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings.

To exercise any right, or to ask a question, write to ffiliugomez@gmail.com.

International transfers

Google may process the advertising data described above on servers outside the European Economic Area, relying on the transfer mechanisms provided for in law. The details are in the Google privacy policy linked above.

Changes to this policy

If what the app handles changes, this page is updated and the date in the header with it. Material changes will also be announced inside the app.
