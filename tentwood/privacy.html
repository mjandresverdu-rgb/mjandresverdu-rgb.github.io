Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Tents and Trees: Cozy Puzzle (“Tents & Trees”) is a logic puzzle game for Android published by LoreApps Studio, contactable at ffiliugomez@gmail.com. It has no user accounts, no server of its own, and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal data.

In short

Your progress stays on your phone. The only data processed is what Google needs to show the ads that pay for the game and, if you choose to buy “Remove ads”, to handle that payment. You decide whether ads are personalised.

What is stored on your phone

Your levels and saved games, daily-puzzle streak, pine cones, campsite decorations and settings (sound, music, vibration, theme, reminder). They stay on your phone, never reach us, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Who else processes data, and why

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address, device model and interaction with ads Showing and measuring ads, and preventing fraud
Google Play Billing Your Google account’s purchase of “Remove ads” Processing the optional one-time payment and restoring it on a new phone. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see them.
Google Play In-App Review Handled entirely by Google Letting you rate the game without leaving it

Advertising

The game is free and funded by Google AdMob ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game we ask whether you accept personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. If you decline, ads will not be personalised. Watching a video ad for a hint or a reward is always optional.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

As we do not keep any personal data, there is nothing of yours for us to access or delete. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights in your Google account. For any question, write to ffiliugomez@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes

If anything described here changes, we will say so on this page with the date of the update.
