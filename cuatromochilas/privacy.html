Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Cuatro Mochilas is an Android travel guide app. It has no user accounts and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal data.

In short

We do not collect any data about you. The only data processed is what Google needs to show ads, and you decide whether those ads are personalised.

What is stored on your phone

Your chosen language, your favourite guides and the tips you have unlocked. They stay on your phone, never reach us, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Who else processes data, and why

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address, device model and interaction with ads Showing and measuring ads, and preventing fraud
Google Firebase IP address and technical request data Downloading the guides, their photos and the app configuration
OpenStreetMap IP address and the map areas requested Downloading the guide maps

Advertising

The app is free and funded by Google AdMob ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the app we ask whether you accept personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in About us → Ad preferences. If you decline, ads will not be personalised.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Location

The app does not use your location. The “Directions” button opens the Google Maps app with the destination set; from then on, Google's policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

As we do not keep any personal data, there is nothing of yours for us to access or delete. For data processed by Google, you can exercise your rights in your Google account. For any question, write to ffiliugomez@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Changes

If anything described here changes, we will say so on this page with the date of the update.
